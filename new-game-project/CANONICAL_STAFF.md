# Canonical starting staff

The main Office Floor begins with **Agent Red plus 19 NPC staff**. Agent Red is the player character and the other 19 are the office cast. This replaces the earlier placeholder roster.

| Character | Job / department | What they notice | Gameplay hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent Red | Industrial espionage operative / player | Chooses what story survives | Completes the contract while pretending to work |
| Bob | Accounting | Numbers and expense inconsistencies | Friendly spreadsheet conversations can trap Red; bad figures attract him |
| Sleepy Steve | IT Support | Passwords, systems, backdoors, computer states | Coffee wakes him; technical favors buy access |
| Pam | HR | Relationships, misconduct, personal business | Can formalize a report or reveal useful secrets |
| Mr Purple | CEO | Executive threat, suspicious absences, company-wide patterns | Roaming boss; staff change behavior when he appears |
| Fran | Finance | Budgets, approvals, missing purchases | Quietly verifies whether Red's spending story is true |
| Chad | Sales | Conversation opportunities and social timing | Extremely easy to distract because he loves talking about himself |
| Rita | Reception | Visitors, badges, arrivals, departures, faces | Best source of identity risk and entrance history |
| Mailroom Mike | Mailroom | Deliveries, keys, routes, overlooked corridors | Can move packages and provide alternate access |
| Dave | Legal | Wording, documents, liability, procedural contradictions | Precise lies work better than dramatic ones |
| Liz | Marketing | Photos, cameras, launches, visual evidence | May create accidental evidence or an accidental distraction |
| Nervous Ned | Security trainee | Noises, unusual events, possible breaches | Raises false alarms; many of his theories are accidentally correct |
| Manager Mo | Operations management | Meetings, calendars, missed work, timing | A human conversation hazard who can consume Red's schedule |
| Jen | Administration | Calendars, rooms, passes, stationery, paperwork | Best source of legitimate reasons to be anywhere |
| Data Dave | Data analysis | Network patterns, databases, repeated incidents | Spots patterns across separate events |
| Boring Bill | Process analysis | Time spent in conversations and process inconsistencies | Can destroy an operation by telling a story with no ending |
| Boss Barbara | Senior management | Inconsistent stories and employee performance | Calmly tests Red's explanation until it breaks |
| Joe | Janitorial | Maintenance problems, back routes, physical changes | Has master access and usually ignores Red unless work gets harder |
| Kevin | Procurement | Inventory, missing gadgets, equipment ownership | Notices when a stolen object is absent from its expected place |
| Old Tom | Senior advisor | Historical scandals, old layouts, forgotten access | Knows what the building used to be and which secrets survived |

## Design rules

- Every NPC has a job, department, movement style, daily hook, primary observation channel, and secondary observation channel.
- Observation specialties modify activation and evidence relevance modestly, never by making one NPC omniscient.
- The same event can be harmless to one person and meaningful to another.
- Staff memories, attitudes, gossip, and reports remain temporary and recoverable unless a major campaign event is created.
- Agent Red can manipulate the office through work, credit-taking, reports, meetings, distractions, disguises, and espionage.
- Mr Purple is the executive roaming threat. Boss Barbara and Manager Mo are management/social threats, not interchangeable security guards.
- A future level designer should author the same fields rather than inventing anonymous generic workers.
